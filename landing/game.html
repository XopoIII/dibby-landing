In short

What is dibby?

dibby is a free, cozy one-finger rope-rescue arcade game, coming soon to iOS and Android. You lower a rope with a single finger, slip past falling rubble, and carry 36 little friends home to a growing camp — in runs of about 30 seconds.

How to play

Understood in five seconds

No tutorial, no tapping lessons — just you, a rope, and someone worth reaching.

1

Hold & guide

The rope tip follows your finger. Sweep left and right to thread the gaps.

2

Go deeper

It falls on its own and speeds up the lower you get. Brush an obstacle and the rope snaps.

3

Bring them home

Reach the bottom, scoop up your friend, and carry them back to the camp.
